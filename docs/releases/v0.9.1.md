# Inkstone 0.9.1

This update makes everyday editing smoother. Existing commands, menus and settings are unchanged; the changes add easier ways to reach them.

## Writing

- **Insert with “/”.** Type `/` at the start of a line to insert headings, lists, tables, code, math, images, a table of contents and more. Filter by English or Chinese name, for example `/table` or `/表`.
- **Front matter stays out of the way.** Documents open below their YAML front matter and title, so neither shows raw syntax. While editing, the metadata is highlighted as YAML, and its collapsed label follows the interface language.
- **Immersive mode.** Press **Ctrl+Shift+Enter**, or choose **View → Immersive mode**, to hide the sidebar, title bar, toolbar and status bar. Press Esc to return.

## Find and navigation

- The find bar shows a match counter (for example `2 / 4`), uses readable labels and toggle buttons for case, regex and whole-word matching, and keeps find and replace in two tidy rows in narrower windows.
- Folder search starts as you type and labels its options clearly.

## Windows of any size

- Below 960 px, the sidebar starts hidden; the sidebar button opens it over the document, and Esc or a click outside closes it.
- Formatting buttons that do not fit move into a “⋯” menu instead of making the toolbar scroll.

## Settings and export

- **Keyboard shortcuts** are recorded by pressing the keys. Conflicts with existing shortcuts are named, and Backspace restores the default. **Text snippets** are edited as a list. JSON editing remains available under Advanced.
- **Typography** offers font presets and sliders for size, line height and writing width that preview while you drag.
- **Pandoc** can be located with a Browse button.
- **Export** starts with PDF, HTML, Word and image choices. The rarely changed header, footer and Pandoc template options are folded into Advanced, and presets are kept below.

## Status and messages

- The status bar names the count clearly (for example “1,424 words · 6 min”) and shows CJK characters and other words separately on hover. Focus and typewriter toggles are highlighted when on.
- Error messages no longer show internal codes such as `CONFLICT:`.
- Before an AI model is configured, the AI panel offers **Set up a model**, and the setup dialog no longer tries to contact a local server just because it opened.

## Validation

On Windows: 210/210 unit tests, 156/156 Electron end-to-end cases (11 new for this release), the browser-level editor suites, and the native packaged-app smoke and interaction tests passed. The new interface was also checked in screenshots in English and Chinese, light and dark themes and narrow windows.
