Brand & app icon kit, v1.0. A free-form inkstone with a drop-shaped ink well: a calm, tactile object for a writing app.

Concept

The stone · 随形砚

Free-form inkstones keep the natural outline of a river stone. The mark is a softly squared pebble, a touch fuller at its base, resting at a slight 8° tilt.

The well · 墨池

Every inkstone has a well where ground ink gathers. Here it is a single drop, set high in the stone like a real well. It is the one thing your eye goes to, much as your writing is in the editor.

The drop in the i

The same drop replaces the dot of the i in the wordmark at the same 8° tilt, so the mark and the name share one shape.

Type

Wordmark

inkstone 墨石

Drawn from Noto Serif SC (Source Han Serif) SemiBold, with Medium for 墨石. Licensed under SIL OFL 1.1, which allows use in logos. Every letter is converted to outlines, so the logo files need no fonts.

Suggested UI pairing

Use the system UI font for app chrome (SF Pro, Segoe UI, PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei). Offer Noto Serif SC or Source Han Serif as a reading font, so the editor sounds like its name.

Usage

Clear space & minimum size

  • Keep clear space around any logo at least the width of the well (the drop).
  • Mark: at least 16 px. Horizontal logo: at least 120 px wide. Stacked logo: at least 64 px wide.
  • Below 32 px, use the -small icon art, not a scaled-down master.

Please don't

  • Recolour the drop or fill it with vermilion. Red drops read as blood.
  • Re-tilt, outline, stretch or add effects to the flat logo. Lighting belongs to the app icon only.
  • Set "inkstone" in another typeface, or swap the drop for a round dot.
